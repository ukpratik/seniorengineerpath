# SeniorPath privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the SeniorPath app for Android (Google Play: `io.github.ukpratik.seniorpath.play`; F-Droid and direct download: `io.github.ukpratik.seniorpath`). The website's policy is on its About page.

## The app collects nothing

The app has no analytics, no ads, no account and no internet permission. Every lesson is inside the app. We do not collect, store or share any personal data, and nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

- **Your progress stays on your phone:** lessons marked done, the last lesson and where you were in it, your learning path and your theme. Exporting a progress file is up to you, and the file goes only where you save it.
- **Android backup.** If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's progress to your own Google account, encrypted. The app never uploads anything itself, and we can't see it.
- **Links** (sources, postmortems, "Report a mistake") open in your browser, under that site's own terms. "Report a mistake" opens GitHub, where what you write is public.

To delete your progress, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app is made for adults learning software engineering. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
