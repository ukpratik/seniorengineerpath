SeniorPath is a free course for software engineers with 2 to 3 years of experience who want to work like senior engineers. It explains system design, distributed systems and software architecture the way they show up at work: how real systems are built, how they break, and how teams fix them. In plain English.

Every lesson has the same eight parts:
• What broke: a real outage or story
• The idea in simple words
• How it works, as a diagram you step through
• Trade-offs
• At work: how a senior engineer uses it
• With AI: how to use coding assistants on it, and where they go wrong
• A short quiz
• Sources to go deeper

WHAT YOU GET
• 124 full lessons on a map of 225 topics, with more in each update.
• Foundations: REST API design, HTTP/2 and HTTP/3, TCP/IP, DNS, TLS, WebSockets, testing, SOLID and clean architecture.
• Data: PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, Cassandra and DynamoDB in depth, transactions and isolation levels, indexes, replication, sharding and consistent hashing.
• Distributed systems: idempotency and retries, the outbox pattern and sagas, CAP and consistency, Raft consensus, event sourcing and CQRS, rate limiting, circuit breakers, stream processing.
• Building at scale: caching and cache stampedes, load balancing, CDNs, microservices or a modular monolith, scaling from zero to millions of users.
• Reliability and security: SLOs and error budgets, incident response and postmortems, safe deploys, OWASP Top 10, authentication and authorization.
• Domains: payments and double-entry ledgers, checkout and inventory, SaaS billing and multi-tenancy, game networking, low-latency systems.
• The senior engineer's craft: design docs, estimation, technical debt, working with product managers, mentoring, system design and behavioural interviews.
• AI-native engineering: LLM basics, prompt and context engineering, RAG, evals, coding agents and reviewing AI-written code.

LEARN BY SEEING IT
• Step-through diagrams: go one step at a time, pinch to zoom, share a step as a picture.
• Six interactive simulations: a cache stampede, replication lag, a staged rollout, a token bucket, a hash ring and a Raft leader election.
• 45 real outages and 56 case studies from companies' own postmortems and engineering blogs, each linked to the original.
• A glossary of 784 terms built into the text: tap any underlined word.
• Learning paths, including "Senior at work" and "Senior interview in 8 weeks", with your next lesson one tap away.
• Search across lessons, terms, outages and case studies.

MADE TO RESPECT YOU
• Works fully offline. The app has no internet permission.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.
• Light and dark themes, large text and screen reader support.

HONEST ABOUT SOURCES
Every lesson cites its sources and every claim links to where it came from. The lessons are written with help from AI tools and checked against those sources. Spotted a mistake? "Report a mistake" at the end of each lesson opens a short form.
